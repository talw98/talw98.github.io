Who I am and what I'm doing

I'm Talha, a PhD researcher based in Paris, France. I started my PhD in December 2024 and I'm currently in my second year, based at ESME Ecole d'Ingenieurs, in affiliation with LISSI Laboratory at UPEC.

My research is in artificial intelligence, mainly deep learning: model design, detection and classification pipelines, and getting systems to hold up under real world constraints. I currently apply this to 3D medical imaging, specifically MRI of liver cancer, working on classification across the main liver tumor types: hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), cholangiocarcinoma (CCA), and mixed HCC-CCA lesions. Much of the work focuses on multi phase MRI: learning jointly across the arterial, portal venous, venous, and delayed phases through phase fusion, as well as reclassification of rarer tumor subtypes per clinical protocol. Before the PhD, I did a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Universite Grenoble Alpes.

Day to day, my work covers the full pipeline: building and validating deep learning models, running proper statistical validation, and publishing the results. That work has led to papers at IEEE BioSMART, SPIE Medical Imaging, and IEEE ISBI, plus a paper accepted at the ECCV 2026 Workshop on AI for Medical 3D Vision (AI4M3D).

I'm starting this blog to write about AI more broadly: my own work and what I'm learning from it, papers I read that I think deserve more attention, and general notes on machine learning and deep learning. I have a fair amount to write about already, so more posts are coming soon.
