Starting this blog: who I am and what I'm researching

I'm Talha, a PhD researcher based in Paris, France. I started my PhD in December 2024 and I'm currently in my second year, working at LISSI Laboratory and ESME Ecole d'Ingenieurs, in partnership with Universite Paris Est.

My research is in artificial intelligence, mainly deep learning, and I apply it to medical imaging. Before the PhD, I did a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at Universite Grenoble Alpes, and before that I worked as a data analyst in clinical settings, which is part of why medical data ended up being where my research focus landed.

Day to day, my work covers the full pipeline: taking raw clinical data, building and validating deep learning models on it, running proper statistical validation, and publishing the results. That work has led to papers at IEEE ISBI, SPIE Medical Imaging, and a preprint currently in preparation for an ECCV workshop.

I'm starting this blog to write about that process in plain terms: what I'm working on, what I'm learning, and occasionally what I got wrong along the way. I have a fair amount to write about already, so more posts are coming soon.
