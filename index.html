01 About

What I work on

I started my PhD in December 2024 and am currently in my second year, based at ESME Ecole d'Ingenieurs in Paris, in affiliation with LISSI Laboratory at UPEC (Universite Paris-Est Creteil). I work on deep learning: designing model architectures, building detection and classification pipelines, and validating them rigorously with proper statistical testing. My current research applies this to 3D medical imaging, but the core of the work is model design, data pipelines, and getting deep learning systems to perform reliably under real world constraints.

Before starting the PhD, I completed a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence at Universite Grenoble Alpes, focused on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and large language models. I care about building AI systems end to end, from raw data through model design and training to validated, published results.
