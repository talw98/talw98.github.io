01 About

What I work on

My research sits at the intersection of medical imaging and deep learning, focused on detecting and characterizing liver tumors from radiological scans. The work spans model design, dataset curation, and the messier realities of clinical data — the parts that rarely make it into a methods section.

Alongside the PhD, I've been deliberately stepping into unfamiliar engineering territory: contributing to open-source infrastructure, working through hackathons, and building the kind of transferable, hands-on skills that pure research doesn't always teach. This site — and its blog — is partly a record of that.

I'm based in Paris and will spend January–February 2027 as a visiting researcher at VU Amsterdam under an AURORA mobility grant.
